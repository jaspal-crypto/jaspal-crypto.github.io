I am an assistant professor of computer science at MBZUAI. My research lies at the intersection of cryptography and privacy, with a focus on designing efficient tools and techniques for secure computation over distributed and private data.

Before joining MBZUAI, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Purdue University and Georgia Tech, hosted by Vassilis Zikas. I received my PhD from Oregon State University, where I was advised by Mike Rosulek. Before that, I completed an MSc at Yale University and a B.Tech. at IIT Ropar.

I'm building my research group and have openings for MSc and PhD students, postdoctoral researchers, and visiting students (3–6 months). You can learn more about my current research interests here. If you're interested, please email me your CV and a brief summary of your research interests, with [Prospective Researcher] in the subject line.
